mcaccel for Horizon 26.3 (version 1.0.0+horizon.1)

A port of Minecraft Accelerated 1.0.0 by Mari.
Original source: https://github.com/Cookieglue/PumpkinsAccelerated
Original licence: CC0-1.0

Ported to Horizon by lavoide: the rules are re-implemented as a Horizon native mod (Rust), and
the content (items, blocks, textures, models, data) is carried over from the original jar.
This port is distributed under the same licence as the original (CC0-1.0); the licence text is
in LICENSE (the original's own file where its jar has one).
