# Show Me What You Got for Horizon

Ctrl+T over an item puts it in chat, with its tooltip on hover.

A port of [Show Me What You Got](https://github.com/apace100/show-me-what-you-got) 1.1.1 by Apace to [Horizon](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon) 26.3, under the original's licence (MIT).

Files (release [`smwyg-v0.1.1`](https://github.com/lavoide/homestead-horizon-ports/releases/tag/smwyg-v0.1.1)), each folder unpacked from its file:

- `smwyg-0.1.1.hnmod` (native source mod): `native/`

Pinned to Horizon `fdc87ae` and Horizon Mixins `e8cd0aa`.
