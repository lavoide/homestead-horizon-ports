# Right Click Harvest for Horizon

Right-click a grown crop to harvest and replant it.

A port of [Right Click Harvest](https://github.com/JamCoreModding/right-click-harvest) 4.6.1+1.20.1 by Jamalam to [Horizon](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon) 26.3, under the original's licence (MIT).

Files (release [`rightclickharvest-v0.1.0`](https://github.com/lavoide/homestead-horizon-ports/releases/tag/rightclickharvest-v0.1.0)), each folder unpacked from its file:

- `rightclickharvest-0.1.0.hnmod` (native source mod): `native/`

Pinned to Horizon `3fb4c31` and Horizon Mixins `e8cd0aa`.
