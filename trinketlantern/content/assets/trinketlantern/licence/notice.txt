trinketlantern for Horizon 26.3 (version 1.0.1)

A port of Wearable lanterns 1.0.1 by tsuki, Absent Abigail.
Original source: https://github.com/AbsentAbigail/WearableLantern
Original licence: MIT

Ported to Horizon by lavoide: the rules are re-implemented as a Horizon native mod (Rust), and
the content (items, blocks, textures, models, data) is carried over from the original jar.
This port is distributed under the same licence as the original (MIT); the licence text is
in LICENSE (the original's own file where its jar has one). It needs Horizon at commit
fdc87aecdbd116bf1e08adcd062fcbaea8c2e2ca.
