comforts for Horizon 26.3 (version 0.1.1)

A port of Comforts 6.4.0+1.20.1 by Illusive Soulworks.
Original source: https://github.com/illusivesoulworks/comforts
Original licence: LGPL-3.0-or-later

Ported to Horizon by lavoide: the rules are re-implemented as a Horizon native mod (Rust), and
the content (items, blocks, textures, models, data) is carried over from the original jar.
This port is distributed under the same licence as the original (LGPL-3.0-or-later); the licence text is
